RECIPE 01

Declarative, for clean REST providers

Pure RestCall declarations — paths, auth placement, schema. No HTTP plumbing: the base connector handles tokens, refresh, and the reauth contract.

sdkSimpleRestConnector(
    slug="acme", base_url="https://api.acme.com",
    calls=[RestCall(name="list_tickets", method="GET", path="/tickets"), …])
